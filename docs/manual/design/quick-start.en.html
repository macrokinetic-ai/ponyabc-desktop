Start Before you start

  1. Install PonyABC Desktop free from the Microsoft Store (scan the code).
  2. Connect the pen with a USB data cable. Bluetooth alone is not enough, and some cables only charge.
  3. Open the app. Home shows “P5 pen connected”. If not, click Rescan.
Microsoft Store

1 Put books on your pen

  1. Open BOOK Library. One line tells you what's new and how long it will take.
  2. Click Sync books. There's no need to choose books.
  3. Keep the pen plugged in. A large book can take about 20 minutes.

1 …then restart the pen

4.  When you see “All done!”, unplug the pen and switch it off and on. The first start takes a little longer while the pen gets its new books ready.
Not enough space? The app checks first and tells you how much room is needed. Back up and delete some recordings, then sync again.

2 Keep your child's recordings

  1. Open My Recordings, then click Back up my recordings. A copy stays on this computer.
  2. To restore, click Put these back on my pen. If a sticker already has a recording, listen to both and choose.
  3. Rename gives a recording a name you'll recognise.

3 Update the pen (Windows)

  1. Open Firmware and follow the steps.
  2. Say Yes when Windows asks for permission.
  3. When it has finished, switch the pen off and on.
Please don't unplug the pen or close the app during the update.

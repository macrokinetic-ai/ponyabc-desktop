### Description (limit 10,000 — used 1,611)

```
PonyABC Desktop is the companion app for the PonyABC P5 talking pen. It does three things, and it tries to do each of them without asking you to understand any of them.

BOOKS, IN ONE BUTTON

Connect the pen with its USB cable and choose Sync books. The app works out which books are missing and which need updating, tells you roughly how long it will take and whether the pen has room, and then gets on with it. There is no list to tick and nothing to choose — and nothing on your pen can be deleted by accident.

The pen reads and writes slowly, so a large book takes a while. You are told that before it starts, not half-way through, and the app counts down while it works.

YOUR CHILD'S RECORDINGS, KEPT SAFE

The recordings your child makes on the pen can be copied to this computer and put back whenever you need them. Give one a name you will recognise, or move it to a different sticker number. If a number already holds a recording, the app plays you both and asks which to keep — it never chooses for you.

UPDATING THE PEN ITSELF (WINDOWS)

When the pen's own software needs updating, the app takes you through it one step at a time and tells you plainly what happened at the end. If an update does not start, your pen is left exactly as it was.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE

English, 繁體中文, 简体中文, Español, Français, Deutsch, Italiano and Português.

A PonyABC P5 pen and its USB cable are needed for anything that touches the pen. You can open the app and look through the book library without one.

Your recordings and books are copied straight between your pen and this computer. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
```

### 完整說明（上限 10,000）

```
PonyABC Desktop 是 PonyABC P5 點讀筆的配套程式。它只做三件事，而且盡量不需要你了解當中的任何細節。

書本：只需一個按鍵

用 USB 線連接點讀筆，然後按「同步書本」。程式會自行判斷缺少哪些書本、哪些需要更新，先告訴你大約需時多久、點讀筆是否有足夠空間，然後才開始。你不需要逐本挑選，也不會誤刪點讀筆內的任何書本。

點讀筆的讀寫速度較慢，較大的書本需要一段時間。這一點會在開始前告訴你，而不是做到一半才說，過程中亦會倒數。

孩子的錄音：妥善保存

孩子在點讀筆錄下的內容，可以複製到這部電腦，日後隨時放回。你可以為錄音改一個自己認得的名稱，或把它移到另一個貼紙號碼。如果該號碼已有錄音，程式會讓你試聽兩段再選擇保留哪一段，不會代你決定。

更新點讀筆本身（Windows）

當點讀筆本身的軟件需要更新時，程式會逐步引導你完成，最後清楚說明結果。如果更新未能開始，點讀筆會維持原狀。

用你的語言

English、繁體中文、简体中文、Español、Français、Deutsch、Italiano、Português。

凡涉及點讀筆的功能，都需要 PonyABC P5 點讀筆及其 USB 線。沒有點讀筆時，仍可開啟程式瀏覽書本資料庫。

你的錄音和書本只在點讀筆與這部電腦之間直接複製，不會上載到任何地方。
```

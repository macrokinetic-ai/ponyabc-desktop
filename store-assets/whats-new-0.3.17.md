# "What's new in this version" — 0.3.17

Paste the matching language into Partner Center → **What's new**. English and 繁體中文 are the
two that matter most; the rest are provided so no market shows an empty field.

**Do not submit anything.** This file is text for a human to paste.

Kept short on purpose: this box is read by parents deciding whether to accept an update, not by
engineers. Every line is something they would notice.

---

## English

```
Books are simpler. One button now puts every new book and update on your pen — no lists to tick,
nothing to choose.

Before it starts, the app tells you how long it will take and checks your pen has room, so you
are never left half-way.

Recordings can now be backed up to your computer and put back on the pen, keeping their sticker
numbers.

Updating your pen is safer: if the update doesn't start, your pen is left exactly as it was.
```
